root@albino:~$ cat about.md

about

I work as a cybersecurity analyst focused on Offensive Security. I do pentesting of Web applications, APIs and Active Directory, exploit and validate vulnerabilities, and write the report that turns each finding into business risk. Manual exploitation comes first: the scanner is the starting point, never the conclusion.

I also work on the defensive side, with EDR, malware and phishing analysis, alert triage, IOC investigation and detection-rule authoring, plus support for ISO/IEC 27001 controls. Understanding how the defense sees is what makes the attack sharper.

I'm an electrical engineer from UFCG. In my thesis I studied attacks on ICS/SCADA networks using the LabShock simulator, exploring Modbus vectors such as Write Coil, MITM and DoS. That's where my interest in infrastructure and IT/OT network security was born — the point where a fault stops being merely digital and becomes physical.

This is where I keep what I produce: write-ups from labs and CTFs, research and technical notes. All of it in my own, authorized or training environments.
